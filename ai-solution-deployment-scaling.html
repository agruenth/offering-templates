What we do

We move AI initiatives from backlog to production — integrating agents and AI-powered workflows into your existing systems, clearing delivery constraints, and enabling operations at scale. We treat agents as engineered systems: observable, maintainable, and native to your ecosystem rather than isolated prototypes.

Approach

  1. Agent Integration: Deploy AI agents into existing workflows and systems. We design for native integration — agents operate within your data boundaries, trigger from real events, and hand off to human or automated processes cleanly.
  2. Backlog Execution: Resolve architectural blockers and accelerate delivery of prioritised use cases. We work through the delivery backlog systematically, with clear sprint goals and acceptance criteria tied to business KPIs.
  3. Operational Enablement: Build for reliability, scalability, and maintainability from day one — covering logging, monitoring, alerting, graceful degradation, and human-in-the-loop escalation paths.
  4. Custom Development & Integration: Build targeted components where out-of-the-box solutions fall short — custom connectors, data transformation layers, orchestration logic, or specialised model fine-tuning.
  5. Vendor Orchestration: Manage external AI providers strategically — defining SLAs, cost controls, fallback strategies, and switching criteria to avoid dependency on any single vendor.

Deliverables

  • Deployed AI Solutions / Agents (production-grade, monitored)
  • Cleared & Executable Delivery Backlog
  • Operational Architecture Setup (observability, alerting, runbooks)
  • Integration & Custom Development Components
  • Vendor Integration Model (SLAs, cost controls, fallback strategies)

Typical engagement

8–16 weeks · 2–5 consultants · Implementation-heavy
