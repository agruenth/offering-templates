What we do

We help technology organisations redesign how they are structured and how they work — moving from project-based, siloed delivery toward a product-centric, team-topology-aware model that reduces coordination overhead and accelerates value delivery.

Approach

  1. Value Stream Mapping: Map the flow of work from idea to production for your top 3–5 value streams. Identify bottlenecks, handoffs, and waste.
  2. Team Topology Assessment: Classify current teams against Team Topologies archetypes (stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated-subsystem). Identify anti-patterns.
  3. Target Operating Model Design: Co-design the future structure with leadership. Define team mandates, interfaces (X-as-a-Service, collaboration, facilitating), and cognitive load boundaries.
  4. Governance & Ways of Working: Design lightweight governance for architecture, security, and data. Align on OKR cadence, planning rituals, and team health metrics.
  5. Transition Planning: Sequence the org changes to minimise disruption. Identify capability gaps and sourcing needs.

Deliverables

  • Current-State Value Stream Maps (with bottleneck analysis)
  • Team Topology Blueprint (future structure, team mandates, interaction modes)
  • Operating Model Design Document
  • Governance Framework (lightweight, outcome-focused)
  • Transition Plan (phased, with change impacts)
  • Job Architecture & Role Profiles for new team types

Typical engagement

8–12 weeks · 2–3 consultants · workshops-heavy
