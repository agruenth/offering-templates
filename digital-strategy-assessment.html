What we do

We run a four-to-six week structured assessment that maps your current digital capabilities against best-in-class benchmarks, surfaces the highest-leverage improvement areas, and delivers a costed, sequenced roadmap your leadership team can act on immediately.

Approach

  1. Discovery (Week 1–2): Structured interviews with 10–20 stakeholders across IT, business units, and leadership. Review of existing strategy documents, architecture diagrams, and KPI dashboards.
  2. Maturity Scoring (Week 3): Score the organisation across six capability dimensions: Data & Analytics, Cloud & Infrastructure, Product & Experience, Organisation & Talent, Governance, and Ecosystem Partnerships.
  3. Opportunity Prioritisation (Week 4): Map findings onto a value-vs-effort matrix. Identify quick wins (0–3 months), medium-term bets (3–12 months), and strategic investments (12–36 months).
  4. Roadmap & Business Case (Week 5–6): Develop initiative briefs with owners, KPIs, budgets, and dependencies. Present to ExCo with recommended sequencing.

Deliverables

  • Digital Maturity Scorecard (6 dimensions, benchmarked against industry peers)
  • Opportunity Register (20–40 prioritised initiatives)
  • 3-Year Digital Roadmap (Gantt + investment view)
  • Executive Presentation deck
  • Business case for top 5 initiatives

Typical engagement

4–6 weeks · 2–3 consultants · Fixed-fee
