What we do

Technology transformations fail because of people, not technology. We design and run the change management workstream that sits alongside every major programme — making sure that people are prepared for the change, supported through it, and empowered to sustain it after go-live.

Approach

  1. Change Impact & Readiness Assessment: Map who is affected, how deeply, and what they need to know, do, and feel differently. Baseline current readiness and resistance.
  2. Stakeholder Engagement Plan: Identify sponsors, champions, and blockers. Design engagement approach per stakeholder group. Brief and coach executive sponsors.
  3. Communication Plan: Design the communication campaign — channels, messages, timing, and feedback loops. Build two-way feedback mechanisms (pulse surveys, town halls, Q&A).
  4. Training & Enablement: Conduct Training Needs Analysis. Design and deliver role-specific learning journeys (e-learning, workshops, coaching, job aids).
  5. Go-Live Support & Sustaining: Hypercare support in the first 30 days. Monitor adoption KPIs. Transition to business-as-usual with embedded change champions.

Deliverables

  • Change Impact & Readiness Assessment
  • Stakeholder Engagement Plan
  • Communications Calendar & Content Pack
  • Training Curriculum (design + delivery)
  • Change Champion Network Setup
  • Adoption Metrics Dashboard
  • Sustaining Plan (post go-live)
