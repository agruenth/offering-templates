What we do

We help organisations create a transparent AI initiative landscape, identify high-value automation and GenAI opportunities across their services, and prepare initiatives for efficient, risk-aware execution — before a single euro is committed to delivery.

Approach

  1. Service Landscape Mapping: Identify and structure existing services and business domains to surface where AI can create the greatest leverage. We map processes, data flows, and decision points across the organisation.
  2. Opportunity Identification: Highlight high-impact automation and GenAI opportunities across processes. Each opportunity is assessed for business relevance, data availability, and implementation complexity.
  3. Capacity & Velocity Assessment: Evaluate current delivery capacity and process speed to understand what the organisation can realistically absorb and execute within a given timeframe.
  4. Pre-flight Risk Mitigation: Define initial risk controls — covering data governance, regulatory exposure, and operational dependencies — before any project is kicked off.

Deliverables

  • AI Opportunity Map (service-based, with impact and feasibility scoring)
  • Prioritised Use Case Overview (top 10–20 opportunities with rationale)
  • Capacity & Velocity Assessment Report
  • Initial Risk Assessment Framework

Typical engagement

2–4 weeks · 1–2 consultants · Interview & workshop driven
