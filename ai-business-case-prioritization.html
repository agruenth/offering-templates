What we do

We translate identified AI opportunities into quantified business cases and define a realistic delivery backlog — sequenced by value, feasibility, and architectural readiness. The result is a funded, defensible programme plan your leadership can commit to with confidence.

Approach

  1. KPI & Benefit Definition: For each candidate initiative, quantify cost-saving and value-creation potential. We model realistic benefit ranges rather than optimistic point estimates, so stakeholder expectations are grounded from the start.
  2. Feasibility Analysis: Assess technical and organisational feasibility — including data readiness, integration complexity, regulatory constraints, and team capability gaps.
  3. Delivery Backlog Creation: Sequence use cases into a scalable delivery roadmap with clear phases, dependencies, and go/no-go decision points. Quick wins are separated from longer-horizon bets.
  4. Capability Alignment: Identify shared platform capabilities and architectural dependencies across initiatives to avoid duplication and enable reuse at scale.

Deliverables

  • KPI-driven Business Case Portfolio (quantified value per initiative)
  • Prioritised AI Use Case Backlog (sequenced delivery plan)
  • Feasibility & Dependency Assessment
  • Capability & Architecture Requirements Overview

Typical engagement

3–6 weeks · 2–3 consultants · Analytics & workshop driven
