What we do

We help you build and adopt an Internal Developer Platform that reduces cognitive load on product teams, enforces good defaults, and gives your developers a self-service experience for infrastructure, pipelines, and environments — without needing to become Kubernetes experts.

Approach

  1. Developer Experience Baseline: Survey developers to understand friction points. Measure DORA metrics (deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, change failure rate) as baseline.
  2. Platform Design: Define the IDP capability map — what golden paths to build first. Design the platform team operating model using Team Topologies principles.
  3. Core Platform Build (Month 1–3): Deploy Kubernetes cluster(s), GitOps toolchain (ArgoCD), developer portal (Backstage), and first golden paths (web service, data pipeline, ML job).
  4. Onboarding & Adoption (Month 3–6): Migrate 3–5 pilot teams onto the platform. Run enablement workshops. Iterate on golden paths based on feedback.
  5. Scale & Operate: Expand to remaining teams. Establish platform team rituals (RFC process, deprecation policy, SLOs for the platform itself).

Deliverables

  • Internal Developer Platform (production-grade, IaC-managed)
  • Developer Portal (Backstage, with service catalogue and golden path templates)
  • 2–4 Golden Path Templates (containerised service, scheduled job, etc.)
  • DORA Metrics Dashboard
  • Platform Team Operating Model & Runbooks
  • Developer Onboarding Guide

Typical engagement

3–6 months initial build · ongoing advisory · 2–4 consultants
