What we do

We help you implement Site Reliability Engineering practices that make reliability a shared engineering responsibility — with clear targets, sustainable on-call, fast incident response, and a culture of continuous improvement.

Approach

  1. Reliability Baseline: Instrument your top 5–10 services with SLIs. Calculate current availability and latency percentiles. Review incident history for patterns.
  2. SLO Workshop: Facilitate cross-functional workshops to define SLOs for each service. Agree error budget policies (when to freeze features vs keep shipping).
  3. Observability Stack: Deploy or improve metrics, tracing, and logging pipelines. Build SLO dashboards. Reduce alert noise — target <5 actionable alerts per on-call shift.
  4. On-Call & Incident Process: Design on-call rotations with sustainable load. Implement incident command structure. Run game days to test runbooks.
  5. Toil Reduction: Identify top toil sources through on-call diaries. Automate or eliminate — target 50% toil reduction in 6 months.

Deliverables

  • SLO Catalogue (per service, agreed with product and engineering)
  • Observability Stack (metrics, traces, logs, SLO dashboards)
  • On-Call Handbook (rotations, escalation, runbooks)
  • Incident Management Process (command structure, comms templates, post-mortem format)
  • Toil Register + Automation Backlog
  • Reliability Score Card (monthly)

Typical engagement

3–6 months · 1–3 consultants · embedded with engineering teams
