What we do

We help engineering organisations build the habits, metrics, and rituals that drive compounding improvement over time — so teams don't just deliver better for a quarter, they keep raising the bar indefinitely.

Approach

  1. Metrics Baseline: Instrument DORA metrics (deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, MTTR) and team health indicators. Establish the current performance profile.
  2. OKR Alignment: Facilitate quarterly OKR setting for engineering, aligned to business goals. Define team-level key results that are measurable and owned.
  3. Improvement Backlog: Run a structured retrospective to surface the top improvement opportunities per team. Prioritise using value-vs-effort and link to OKRs.
  4. Cadence & Rituals: Design the improvement rhythm — weekly team retros, monthly leads forum, quarterly DORA review. Coach facilitators.
  5. Sustaining: Build internal coaching capability. Hand over dashboards, facilitation guides, and OKR templates. Quarterly health check for 12 months.

Deliverables

  • DORA Metrics Dashboard (live, per team and org-wide)
  • Team Health Check Scorecard
  • OKR Framework & Templates
  • Improvement Backlog (prioritised, owned)
  • Retrospective Facilitation Guide
  • Engineering Excellence Playbook
  • Quarterly Review Cadence Setup

Typical engagement

3 months setup · 12-month quarterly advisory · 1–2 consultants
