What we do

We conduct a thorough, independent review of your technology landscape — applications, data flows, infrastructure, and operating practices — and produce an actionable target-state architecture with a migration path that balances risk, cost, and speed.

Approach

  1. Inventory & Discovery: Catalogue all applications, integrations, and infrastructure components. Collect architecture decision records and existing documentation.
  2. Risk & Debt Analysis: Score each component on technical debt, operational risk, business criticality, and strategic fit. Identify the top 10 risk hotspots.
  3. Target Architecture Design: Co-design the target state with your architects using C4 models. Evaluate build vs buy vs SaaS options for key capabilities.
  4. Migration Sequencing: Produce a phased transition plan with clear milestones, cost estimates, and dependency mapping.

Deliverables

  • Application Portfolio Assessment (heatmap + scoring)
  • Current-State Architecture Diagrams (C4 Level 1–3)
  • Target-State Architecture (C4 Level 1–3)
  • Technology Debt Register
  • Migration Roadmap with cost/risk/value per initiative
  • Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) for key choices

Typical engagement

6–8 weeks · 2–4 consultants · Fixed-fee or T&M
