What we do

We design the target AI architecture, evaluate vendors and models, and establish a structured decision framework that balances cost, risk, and data sovereignty — so your organisation builds on a foundation it can own, govern, and evolve over time.

Approach

  1. Vendor Capability Assessment: Evaluate existing and potential vendors across model quality, integration maturity, pricing models, support, and strategic alignment. Includes both hyperscaler AI services and specialist providers.
  2. Cost vs. Risk Analysis: Derive the optimal sourcing strategy by modelling total cost of ownership against risk exposure — covering data residency, contractual lock-in, and operational dependency.
  3. Target Architecture Design: Define a modular and scalable AI architecture covering model access, orchestration, data pipelines, observability, and security controls — designed to support multiple use cases without rebuilding from scratch each time.
  4. Risk & Sovereignty Evaluation: Address regulatory requirements (GDPR, AI Act, sector-specific rules), security boundaries, and vendor lock-in risks with concrete mitigation strategies.

Deliverables

  • Target AI Architecture Blueprint (component-level, with rationale)
  • Vendor Assessment & Selection Framework
  • Cost/Risk Trade-off Analysis (build vs. buy vs. partner)
  • Sovereignty & Compliance Evaluation

Typical engagement

4–8 weeks · 2–3 consultants · Architecture & decision-focused
